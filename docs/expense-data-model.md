# Travel and expense data model

Travel requests and claims use tenant-scoped resources. Each record has an owner, a state and a revision. A submission freezes its routing policy and calculation evidence. The immutable version hash is included in decision audit events. Submitted lines have stable UUIDs across edits, the rule and policy revision effective on the service date, and exact decimal claimed and allowed amounts. Final finance allowances are stored separately for each submitted line. An edited draft leaves prior versions and decisions intact.

The expense workspace and its commands require an active organization membership and a live email-and-password session. Employees can create and see their own records. Approval and finance actions require explicit assignment under the published route. The requester cannot approve, finance cannot approve after acting as the manager, and a missing or inactive route stays pending until reassigned. Scoped administrators can set employee expense grades in the expense workspace. The administration settings define the effective policy, limits and reviewers. Expense submissions fail when no service-dated rule applies. Development tests use synthetic policy and evidence.

Advance issuance, application and return, reimbursements, and reimbursement reversal entries are append-only. Finance posts and reconciles each manually with private scanned evidence. Issuing or returning an advance locks the trip. Applying or reversing an applied advance locks both the claim and trip. Reimbursement locks the claim, checks its current outstanding balance and serializes concurrent attempts with optimistic revision checks. The operation receipt makes same-key retries idempotent. Finance's scoped CSV uses approved-version and stable line IDs and repeats claim-level totals only on its first line. Exports are audited. The application does not initiate transfers or book transactions in Zoho.

Ship the database migration before the expense workspace. To roll back a release, restore the previous application build and retain the expense tables. Correct discrepancies with an audited reversal or a forward migration. Retain drafts, submitted data and evidence when recovering service.

Local verification covers the secure workflow, decimal calculations, duplicate warnings, revision preservation, reviewer independence, evidence checks, advance accounting, reimbursement and reversal, idempotency and finance export. A separate test targets concurrent payment requests through real local Auth and PostgREST and uses synthetic scanner status. Hosted scanner, actual AVI expense policy, expense UAT, temporary delegation and automatic approval notifications require separate verification or implementation.
