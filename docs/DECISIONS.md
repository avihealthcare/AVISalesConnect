### Field Operations GPS and task settings (2026-11-28)

The Field Operations request sets the visit radius default to 500 metres. The organization can set it from 25 to 10,000 metres and toggle each task automation. Locations are collected only after the user presses Capture Location for a punch or visit check-in or check-out. Unavailable, stale or insufficient readings and site mismatches require an exception reason. The location retention approval in SRS SEC-08 remains the production gate.

Field Operations views use Asia/Kolkata calendar days, including attendance, visit plans and daily reports. One active working day/visit is allowed per employee. Calendar weekdays after the first recorded shift show Absent when no punch or correction exists. Leave and holidays can be recorded through the approved correction workflow. Task retries use versioned writes and deterministic unique keys. A tender reminder is due one day before its deadline and a payment reminder is due one day before its due date.
